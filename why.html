Why FigAMP?

Most amp plugins make you choose: a giant toolkit that takes an evening to learn, a signature pack built for one style, or a free capture you have to build a rig around. FigAMP is a complete, great-sounding guitar rig you can learn in five minutes, for less than half the usual price.

01 · Fast

Great tone in one click

Every amp has a Classic Tone button that loads its sweet spot with the right cabinet. No signal chains to build, no menus to dig through. Plug in, pick an amp, play.

02 · Range

Seven amps, every style

Sparkling American cleans, chiming class-A, British crunch, hot-rodded rock and liquid high-gain lead, all in one plugin. Not a single-genre pack.

03 · Studio chain

Console preamps built in

Four studio console preamp flavours sit in front of the amp: Brit 73, NY 312, Brit 4K and London A. That's the "recorded through a great desk" sound most amp plugins leave to separate, expensive plugins.

04 · Width

Micro Shift doubler

A stereo pitch-shift doubler for huge, wide rhythm parts without double-tracking, alongside a compressor pedal, tempo-synced delay and a room reverb.

05 · Feel

Modelled from the circuit

FigAMP models each amp's gain stages, tone stack and power amp, so every knob reacts the way the real amp does. Captures are fixed snapshots; FigAMP stays fully tweakable.

06 · Fair

$49, yours to keep

A one-time purchase. No subscription, no USB dongle, no bundle to buy into. Try every feature free for 14 days first.
