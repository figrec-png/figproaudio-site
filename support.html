Installing

Mac

Unzip the download and double-click FigAMP-1.2.2.pkg. The installer is signed and notarized by Apple, so there are no security warnings. It asks for your Mac password and installs the Audio Unit, VST3 and standalone app into the standard folders. Then restart your DAW. The loose FigAMP.component, FigAMP.vst3 and FigAMP.app are also in the zip if you prefer to copy them by hand.

Windows

Unzip the download and run FigAMP-1.2.2-Setup.exe. It installs FigAMP.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and the standalone app into Program Files › Fig Audio › FigAMP, with a Start menu entry and an uninstaller. No separate Microsoft runtime download is needed. If Windows shows the blue "Windows protected your PC" screen, click More info, then Run anyway. Then restart your DAW and rescan plugins if it asks.

Activating

How do I activate my licence?

Open FigAMP and click Buy / Activate in the top right. Sign in with the account you used to buy, and FigAMP activates on that computer. You can also activate offline with a machine file from your account page.

What happens when the trial ends?

FigAMP passes your guitar through unprocessed until you activate a licence. Your sessions and settings are kept.

Common questions

The standalone app doesn't hear my guitar

On a Mac, allow microphone access: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone, switch FigAMP on, then reopen the app. In the app's audio settings, choose your interface and the input your guitar is plugged into.

My DAW doesn't show FigAMP

Restart the DAW after installing and run its plugin rescan. On a Mac, LUNA and Logic use the Audio Unit version; most other DAWs use VST3.

How do I save my own presets?

Dial in a sound, open the Preset menu and choose Save preset…. Give it a name and it appears under My presets in the same menu, in the plugin and the standalone app. Delete preset and Show presets folder are right below. Presets are small files in Library › Audio › Presets › Fig Audio › FigAMP on a Mac and %APPDATA%\Fig Audio\FigAMP\Presets on Windows, so you can back them up or copy them to another computer.

What does the Compressor do?

It's a pedal-style compressor in front of the amp. Switch it on, turn Sustain up for longer notes and more even picking, and use Level to set how hard the signal hits the amp (5 is about unity). Factory presets leave it off; your own presets remember it.

Can I use my own cabinet impulse responses?

Yes. Click Load IR… in the Cabinet section and choose a .wav file.
