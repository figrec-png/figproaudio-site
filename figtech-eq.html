The analog panel

  • Drive. How hard the tubes and transformers are driven. 50 % is +4 dBu at -18 dBFS, the level the hardware expects.
  • Input from -12 to +12 dB into the unit.
  • Mix. Blend dry and processed, phase aligned, for parallel EQ.
  • Width of the output, from 0 to 200 %.
  • Analog pair variance. Each channel gets its own component tolerances and tube match, fixed per instance, like a real pair of units.
  • Tube hum and noise: the hardware's very low mains hum and hiss, if you want it.
  • A live frequency response so you see what the knobs are doing.

Built for the mix

  • Link, Dual and Mid/Side. One set of controls for both channels, separate left and right, or separate mid and side. Linked or not, each channel has its own circuit.
  • EQ IN / OUT takes the equalizer out and leaves the tubes and transformers in circuit, as on the hardware. POWER off is a true bypass, latency compensated.
  • Oversampling for playback (2x by default) and a separate setting for bounces (4x by default), with minimum-phase or linear-phase filters.
  • A/B compare, and copy and paste settings between instances.
  • Your own presets in folders by category, next to the factory set.
