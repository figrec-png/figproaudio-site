A whole studio chain

  • Console preamps. Four studio preamp flavours in front of the amp: Brit 73, NY 312, Brit 4K and London A.
  • Cabinets. Six matched cabinets with a mic position control, or load your own impulse responses.
  • Delay. Syncs to your session tempo, with warm, darkening repeats and ping-pong.
  • Room. From a small studio room to a big hall.
  • Compressor. A pedal-style compressor in front of the amp, with Sustain and Level, for even picking and longer sustain. New in 1.2
  • Micro Shift. A stereo pitch-shift doubler for wide, thick rhythm parts.
  • Noise gate and Stereo/Mono switch. Quiet high gain, and the right width for any track.
